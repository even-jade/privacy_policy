Yeohaengcheop Account & Data Deletion

App: Yeohaengcheop (여행첩, formerly TripTable, Android) · Developer: Yoni.Gyun · Last updated: 9 October 2026
Privacy Policy · 한국어 (정본 / authoritative version)

Summary

· If you have the App, delete right away in Settings (설정) ▸ Cloud storage (보관) ▸ Delete account (계정 지우기).

· If you have already uninstalled the App, email yonigyun@gmail.com. Your data will be deleted and you will be notified within 10 days of receipt.

· If you never connected an account and never joined shared editing, there is no data on the server to delete.

The App's interface is in Korean. Korean labels are shown in parentheses so you can find them on screen. In case of any discrepancy, the Korean version of this page is the authoritative text.

1. First, check whether there is anything on the server

Yeohaengcheop stores your trips and photos on your device. A copy and an account are created on the Yeohaengcheop server only if you connected a Google account in Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관), or joined someone else's trip for shared editing with an invite code. If you did neither, there is no data on the server to delete, and the data on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Delete in the App

  1. Open Yeohaengcheop and tap ⋮ (Settings, 설정) at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Tap Cloud storage (보관).
  3. Tap Delete account (계정 지우기) at the bottom, then tap Delete account (계정 지우기) again in the confirmation dialog.
  4. If you connected a Google account and have not signed in within the last few minutes (the usual case), an account chooser appears so you can confirm it is you. Choose the same account and deletion continues.

Your account is deleted only after the server cleanup finishes. If it cannot finish — for example because the connection drops — your account is not deleted and the App tells you so. Check your connection and try again.

3. Request deletion without the App

If you have already uninstalled the App or cannot delete from within it, send an email as below. There is no separate form; requests are accepted by email.

  • To: yonigyun@gmail.com
  • Subject: Yeohaengcheop data deletion request
  • Send from: the email address of the Google account you connected to Yeohaengcheop. That address is used to find your account and to confirm the request comes from you. If you write from a different address, we may ask for further verification.

Your data will be deleted within 10 days of receipt, and the result will be sent to the address you wrote from. If verification is needed, the period counts from when verification is complete. Your request email is used only to process the request and report the result.

If you joined shared editing anonymously

If you joined only with an invite code, without connecting a Google account, that account has no name or email, so there is no way to verify you without the App. In that case the server holds only your membership records and the changes and photos you added in that trip. You can:

  • Delete in the App as in section 2, if you still have it.
  • Ask the person who created the trip to end shared editing (함께 편집 끝내기). This deletes the trip's membership records.
  • Otherwise, email the address above with the trip you joined and roughly when. We will act to the extent we can verify and let you know the result.

4. Data that is deleted

  • Your trip records on the server — trip name, destination and dates, itinerary items and notes, accommodation and transport details
  • Photo files and photo information for your trips on the server
  • Invite codes you created, and the membership records of companions in your trips
  • Your membership records in other people's trips (including your display name)
  • Your account in the authentication service — account identifier, your Google account's email address and basic profile, and sign-in records. For the identifier left in companions' trips, see section 5

5. Data that remains

  • Trips and photos on your device — account deletion removes only the server copy. Data on your device is removed when you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Changes you made and photos you added in a companion's trip — these remain part of that trip, together with the random account identifier recorded on them, until that trip or photo is deleted. To remove those photos, delete them in the App before deleting your account.
  • Records already downloaded to a companion's device — companions can no longer see your trip, but copies they already downloaded stay on their devices.
  • Anonymous identifier — if you joined anonymously, your membership records on the server are deleted, but if the authentication service requires you to re-verify, there is no way to do so for an anonymous account, and an anonymous identifier containing no personal information may remain in the authentication service. No name, email or other information that identifies you is linked to it, and it may remain without a set retention period.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports — these are not linked to your account, so account deletion does not remove them. They are deleted automatically when their retention period expires (statistics up to 14 months, crash reports up to 90 days).
  • Files you exported — these live outside the App and must be deleted by you.
