Summary

· No sign-up needed to use the App. We do not ask for your name or phone number.

· Your trips, photos and notes are stored on your device. Unless you connect an account or join shared editing, they never leave your device.

· If you connect a Google account, your trips and photos are kept in cloud storage on the Yeohaengcheop server, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same account, and you can edit a trip together with companions you give an invite code to. The server runs on Google and Cloudflare infrastructure.

· You can delete your account in the App at any time, or request deletion without the App on the deletion request page.

· The App never asks you for device permissions while running. It only receives photos you pick yourself in the system photo picker.

· Location is resolved to country/region only — not your city or device model.

· We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the App.

· We show no ads and we do not sell your information.

Yoni.Gyun ("the Developer") operates the Yeohaengcheop mobile application, formerly TripTable ("the App"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. In case of any discrepancy, the Korean version is the authoritative text.

1. Information We Do Not Collect, and Data on Your Device

The App does not collect:

  • Your name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth — the App never asks you for them. If you connect an account, section 2 explains how your Google account's email address and name are handled
  • Access to your contacts, photo library, files, microphone, or camera — the App requests none of these permissions. Photos reach the App only through the Android system photo picker, and only the photos you choose; the App cannot read your photo library
  • Precise (GPS) location

Your trip, photo and note data

Trip names, destinations, dates, itinerary items, notes, accommodation and transport details that you enter, and the photos you attach to your itinerary, are stored in your device's local storage.

If you do not connect an account and do not join shared editing, this data is never transmitted to the Developer's servers or to any third party. In that case the App does not even create an account on the server, and the Developer cannot access your data. Section 2 describes what is sent when you connect an account or join shared editing with an invite code.

How photos are handled

  • Photos reach the App only through the Android system photo picker, and only the ones you choose. No storage permission is requested.
  • The App stores a downsized copy of each chosen photo in its private storage. The original is not kept, and the original in your gallery is neither modified nor deleted.
  • When making the copy, the App reads only the capture time and orientation from the photo's metadata (EXIF). Location (GPS) and all other metadata are not carried into the copy.
  • If you connect an account, this copy is what is uploaded. Original photos and their capture location are never sent to the server.

Automatic backup

If you have Android's automatic backup enabled, your trips and notes may be backed up to your own Google account. That transfer takes place between you and Google; the Developer has no access to those backups. Photo copies are excluded from automatic backup. To move photos to another device, use the export feature described below or the cloud storage described in section 2.

Export and import

The export feature in Settings bundles your trips, notes and photo copies into a single file (zip) saved to a location you choose in the Android system file picker. Import reads a file you choose and adds its trips to the App. Both happen entirely on your device and never pass through the Developer's servers. You are in control of storing, sharing and deleting exported files; because they contain your trip data in full, take care when sharing them with others.

The "save as image" feature writes the generated image to your device's gallery only. It is not uploaded anywhere. It leaves your device only if you explicitly choose to share it, and only to the app you select.

2. If You Connect an Account — Cloud Storage and Shared Editing

Cloud storage and shared editing are optional features you choose to use. They are not required to use the App; without them, trip planning and the photo album work exactly the same.

When you connect a Google account in Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관), all trips on this device and any trips you create afterwards are kept on the Yeohaengcheop server and become visible on your other devices signed in to the same account. The following is stored on the server:

DataPurposeRetention
Trip records: trip name, destination and dates, itinerary items and notes, accommodation and transport details Cloud storage, viewing on your other devices, shared editing Until you delete your account. A trip you delete is removed at that time (exceptions in section 7)
Photos (the downsized copies described in section 1) and photo information (which trip and item a photo belongs to, capture time, caption, time added, and the account identifier of the uploader) As above As above. A photo you delete is removed at that time

Display name — only if you join someone else's trip for shared editing while your Google account is connected Showing your companions who is taking part Until you leave that trip, shared editing ends, the trip's creator deletes the trip or their account, or you delete your account
Invite codes and the trip each code points to Letting a companion join shared editing An invite code expires and stops working 7 days after it is created. It is deleted when revoked, when shared editing ends, or when you delete your account

Your email address is not stored in your trip records or in anything your companions can see. It is held by the authentication service so that you can sign in, and is deleted together with your account.

While requests are exchanged, each provider's infrastructure processes your IP address. The Developer does not separately record or keep it.

Joining shared editing anonymously

You can join a trip for shared editing with an invite code from a companion without connecting a Google account. In that case the App creates an anonymous account identifier, with no name or email, on the server and records it as a member of that trip. What is uploaded is the changes you make and the photos you add in that trip, plus any trips you create or import on this device after joining (including their photos), which are kept under that anonymous account. Trips that were already on your device before you joined are not uploaded.

An anonymous account cannot be recovered once the App is uninstalled, and because it has no name or email it is hard to verify you without the App. Please use Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관) ▸ Delete account (계정 지우기) before uninstalling.

Who can see your data

  • Your own devices signed in to the same Google account
  • Companions you personally gave an invite code to — for that trip only. Invite codes are 12 characters long, expire after 7 days, and can be revoked by their creator at any time
  • No one else. Photos have no public URL, and the server checks on every request that the requester is a member of the trip. That check is reused for up to one minute, so for one minute after someone loses access (shared editing ends, they leave, or an account is deleted) they may still be able to download or upload that trip's photos

When shared editing ends

When the person who created a trip ends shared editing, its invite codes are revoked and companions can no longer see the trip. Records already downloaded to a companion's device remain on that device.

Retention

The server copy is kept until you delete your account. Uninstalling the App does not remove the server copy. To remove it, use Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관) ▸ Delete account (계정 지우기) before uninstalling, or, if you have already uninstalled the App, follow the deletion request page. Section 6 describes what deletion does.

3. Information Collected Automatically

To improve the App and fix errors, the following is collected automatically through Google LLC's Firebase services, whether or not you connect an account.

These records never contain the content of your trips. The values transmitted are content-free metrics such as "an itinerary item was added", "an image was saved", "N photos were added", "how many days a trip spans", or "an account was connected". Text you enter — trip names, destinations, notes — and the photos themselves are never sent in any field. The account identifier and email address described in section 2 are not included either.

Granular location and device data is not collected

Google Analytics' "granular location and device data collection" setting is turned off. As a result, the following are not collected:

  • City-level location, latitude and longitude
  • Device brand, device model, device name
  • Screen resolution
  • Minor versions of the operating system and platform

Location is resolved only to country and region level; the city you are in is neither collected nor stored. The App requests no permission to access your device's location services such as GPS.

6. Retention, Deletion and Account Deletion

Data described in section 3 is deleted automatically by Google once the retention period listed there expires. Trips, notes and photo copies stored on your device are deleted when you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Files you created with the export feature live outside the App and must be deleted by you.

When you delete your account

Use Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관) ▸ Delete account (계정 지우기) in the App. If you have already uninstalled the App or cannot delete from within it, follow the deletion request page.

  • Your trip records and photos on the server, and your account (including your email and other sign-in data), are deleted
  • Trips and photos on this device remain. The App returns to its pre-connection state
  • Companions you were editing with can no longer see the trip. Records already downloaded to their devices remain on those devices
  • Changes you made and photos you added in a companion's trip remain part of that trip. The random account identifier recorded on them remains too, and is deleted when that trip or photo is deleted. To remove those photos, delete them in the App before deleting your account
  • If the server cleanup cannot finish, your account is not deleted and the App tells you so. Check your connection and try again
  • If you joined shared editing anonymously, your membership records on the server are deleted, but if the authentication service requires you to re-verify, there is no way to do so for an anonymous account, and an anonymous identifier containing no personal information may remain in the authentication service. No name, email or other information that identifies you is linked to it, and it may remain without a set retention period
  • Section 3 statistics and crash records are not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not delete them; they are deleted when their retention period expires

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can exercise the following directly:

  • Block the advertising identifier used for statistics — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (the path may be Settings → Google → Ads on some devices)
  • Stop all collection — uninstall the App. Note that the server copy remains after uninstalling, so also delete your account as below
  • Delete the server copy and your account — Settings ▸ Cloud storage (보관) ▸ Delete account (계정 지우기), or without the App via the deletion request page
  • Leave shared editing — from the menu of a trip you joined. The person who created a trip can end shared editing or revoke its invite code
  • Delete your trip data — delete trips or photos in the App, or uninstall the App. If you have connected an account, trips and photos you delete in the App are deleted from the server as well. However, if you delete a trip that has been cut off from the server (shown in the App as 떨어져 나온 여행), or if deleting a photo file fails, the server copy may remain until that trip is deleted or you delete your account
  • Take your data with you — use Export in Settings to save your trips, notes and photos as a file

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To make such a request, contact the Developer at the address in section 9. The Developer will act on your request and reply within 10 days of receiving it.

Please note: the data in section 3 is processed in statistical form and keyed to identifiers that do not directly identify you. Locating or deleting one individual's records may therefore be technically impracticable. Where that is the case, the Developer will act to the extent verification is possible and will explain the limitation.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

The Developer does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. California residents may exercise their rights to know, delete, and correct personal information, and will not be discriminated against for doing so, by contacting the Developer at the address in section 9.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 14, or such higher age as may be required by the law applicable to you, and the Developer does not knowingly collect their personal information. If the Developer learns that such information has been collected, it will be deleted promptly. If you believe a child's information has been collected, please contact the Developer.

10. Security

  • Section 3 data and section 2 cloud storage and shared editing data are encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Trip records on the server can be read and written only by members of that trip, enforced by access rules
  • Photo files are kept in private storage with no public URL. The relay server verifies your sign-in and checks trip membership on each request before serving a photo
  • On your device, trip data and photo copies are stored inside the Android application sandbox and are not accessible to other apps
  • Photo copies carry no location or other metadata from the original
  • Apart from the section 2 data needed to provide the service, the Developer keeps no separate store of information that could identify you

The policy will also be updated before any expansion of what is collected — for example, if advertising is ever introduced into the App.
